Questions

What is Endless Tales?

Finished audio drama series — full casts, scored sound and seven episodes per story. Made for driving, working out, walking or winding down.

How long is each episode?

Most run between twenty and thirty-five minutes. A full series is roughly three to four hours of listening.

Do I need to wait for new episodes?

No. Every series is written, voiced and mixed before it is published, so the whole story is waiting when you start.

Can I read the stories too?

Yes. Every story is also an eBook in the app, included with your subscription.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Episodes can be downloaded in the app for flights, tunnels and dead zones.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from your account page, in a couple of taps. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
