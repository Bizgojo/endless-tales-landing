Questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of full access to every story. A card is needed to start. Cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing. We'll remind you before the trial ends.

Can I read instead of listen?

Yes. Every story comes as audio and as an ebook. Listen in the car, read in bed, or switch whenever you like.

Where can I listen?

Anywhere your phone goes. Earbuds at the gym or on a walk, your car's Bluetooth on the road, or a speaker at home. Episodes continue on their own, so you never have to look at a screen.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Endless Tales works in your phone's web browser.

How long is an episode?

Most run about 12 to 18 minutes. Long enough for a drive or a workout, short enough for a break.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in a few taps.
